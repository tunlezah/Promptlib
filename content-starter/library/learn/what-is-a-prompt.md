A **prompt** is the text you send to an AI model. That is all. It can be a question, an instruction, a document with a request at the end, or a long set of rules.

The model reads the prompt and writes a reply. It has no memory of you beyond what is in the conversation, and it cannot see your screen, your files or your intentions. Everything it knows about your task, it knows from the prompt.

## Why some prompts work better

A good prompt tells the model four things:

1. **Who it is** — "You are a patient tutor", "You are a senior engineer". This sets the tone and the level of detail.
2. **What to do** — the task, in one or two plain sentences.
3. **What to work on** — the text, code or data. Paste it after the instructions, or use a fill-in field.
4. **What the answer should look like** — a list, a table, a length limit, headings to use.

Most prompts in this library are built that way. Read a few and you will start to see the pattern.

## How to use a prompt from this library

1. Open a prompt. If it has **fill-in fields**, type your values into them. The text updates as you type.
2. Press **Copy**.
3. Paste into your chat app, or use **Open in** if your app is listed.
4. If the prompt ends with "Paste your text below" or similar, paste your material after it and send.

If the reply is not what you wanted, tell the model what was wrong and ask again. That follow-up is itself a prompt.
